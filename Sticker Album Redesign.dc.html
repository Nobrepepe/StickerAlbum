Rules
  • The result is already committed before the view opens (open_pack saves first), so every beat must be skippable — “Reveal all” jumps straight to the last card with no animation debt.
  • One page.run_task coroutine owns the whole sequence; it checks control.page is not None after each sleep, like _settle_stamp already does, so navigating mid-reveal can’t explode.
  • Stagger with asyncio.sleep between property sets — Flet has no per-child delay.

  • Sound: stamp.wav on land, a paper-tear cue on beat 2, the sticker’s own voice line on beat 4 (reuse play_stamp_then), play_spicy unchanged.

0Brief — what I think is wrong, and the plan
The real problem

The white board isn’t the problem — the surround is. Right now it’s a cool blue-grey panel (#2a2530-ish) with cool grey empty slots, so pure white lands on it as a harsh cut-out. Two fixes that don’t touch your images: make the app surround warm (walnut, kraft, bone) so white reads as paper rather than a hole, and give the board a physical edge — a page shadow, a gutter, a mat — so the eye reads “sheet resting on desk” instead of “div with a white background”.

Empty slots

Your masked previews are the right idea, wrong value. Flat mid-grey is the single biggest source of “generic” in the screenshots — it makes 40% of the page look broken. Same mask, but rendered as a pale pencil silhouette: near-white fill, faint hatch, thin dashed outline. The unique silhouette still previews, the board still blends, and empty reads as “not yet” rather than “missing asset”.

Other things I’d change
  • Rarity is currently five saturated pill colors fighting the art. Encode it in material instead — plain label, gold-edged label, holo label — one hue for the whole app.
  • The sidebar is generic stock chrome. It should feel like the album’s spine: warm, narrow, quiet.
  • The four stat boxes on Home read as a dashboard. Savings stays, but as one line of type, not a KPI grid.
  • Pack opening deserves the motion budget: a real pack, a tear, cards sliding out, foils catching light. That’s the moment people replay.
Assumptions

Flet-buildable: no exotic CSS, effects achievable with containers, shadows, gradients and images. Art below is placeholder blobs — organic splashes on white standing in for your stickers, so I can show the blending without pretending to redraw your work. Desktop-first, 1180px content width. Once the repo is in I’ll rebuild these as your real components.
